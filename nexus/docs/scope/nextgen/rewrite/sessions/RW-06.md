## Engine decision (roadmap §8 — DataFusion-first)

Implemented the vectorized backend on **DataFusion** (already in-tree, Arrow-native, no
second Arrow stack). Polars was NOT adopted: every first-set primitive maps to a DataFusion
window/aggregate/projection expression over a `SessionContext`, and `resample` is `date_bin`
+ group-by (the Timescale-shaped path). Adopting Polars would add polars-arrow/arrow2 — the
exact dep-bloat §8 forbids — for no ergonomic win. No new heavy dep beyond `rhai` (§8-approved).
