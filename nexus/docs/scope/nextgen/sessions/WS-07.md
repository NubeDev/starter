## Assumptions

- Email uses `lettre` (rustls TLS, no native-tls) — added as a nexus-api dependency.
- SMTP/Slack credentials live in the channel `config` jsonb. The design doc notes the R6
  envelope is for *authenticated* secrets; full envelope-encryption of channel secrets is
  noted as a follow-up (see TODOs) since the channel store has no secret column yet and
  adding one couples with the datasource envelope wiring — out of a single session's safe
  scope. Tokens are never returned to the client (config is redacted on read).
